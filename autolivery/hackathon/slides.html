Autolivery

An agent that paints race cars.

Give it a brief. It designs a livery, fits it to the car's real 3D model, and revises until the checks pass. A person accepts it.

Fitment is a verification tool the agent cannot talk its way past: millimetres, against the car's geometry.

liverykit · autoliveryAI Infra Summit hackathon · September 2026

The loop doesn't know it's a car. The car is in what you plug into it.

Measured, not judged

Fitment measures, in millimetres

  • Letter height on the car: a number at least 140 mm, a name 45 mm
  • Margin from every edge, shut line and door handle
  • A name's contrast against what is under it, 6:1
  • A stripe's direction, alignment and gaps, panel to panel
  • How much of each piece every view actually shows

The critic judges, from pictures

  • Does it read as the style the brief named
  • Is each thing the brief asked for present, and where
  • Composition and taste, which is what a model is for
  • A second look, closer, when the two disagree

Anything the model can compute is measured. The critic only answers what a picture can show.
